# Code of Conduct

BridgeX is committed to building a respectful, professional, and collaborative development environment.

## Our Standards

- Be respectful.
- Be professional.
- Accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on solving problems.
- Respect different opinions.
- Keep discussions technical and productive.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment
- Discrimination
- Personal attacks
- Offensive language
- Spam
- Unauthorized disclosure of confidential information

## Enforcement

Project maintainers reserve the right to remove or restrict access to contributors who violate this code of conduct.
