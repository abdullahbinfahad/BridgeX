## Cover

BridgeX

Global goods-carrying marketplace

Connecting senders with verified travelers and available carry capacity

## Slide 1

### Cross-border delivery is a matching problem

- Senders need route-specific, personal-scale delivery support
- Travelers often have unused luggage or cargo capacity
- BridgeX creates a controlled place for discovery and protected matching

## Slide 2

### Public discovery. Protected execution.

- Guests can explore public requests and carry space
- Members post, respond, and manage their own activity
- Addresses, contact details, payment proof, and direct chat are released only through a protected match

## Slide 3

### Two-sided marketplace, one clear workflow

- **I need to send:** post an item, route, timing, weight, handling, and budget
- **I have carry space:** post capacity, route, dates, categories, and transport details
- Offers and interests bring the two sides together

## Slide 4

### Trust is built through visible controls

- Identity verification and public verification signals
- Real ratings from completed protected orders
- Reporting, administrator review, and restricted-account actions

## Slide 5

### Payment proof protects the match opening

- A selected response creates a protected payment request
- The sender uploads proof for administrator review
- The protected match and private details open only after verification

## Slide 6

### Delivery and payout have a recorded end state

- Traveler updates the delivery lifecycle
- Sender confirms receipt and releases the completed order
- Traveler payout becomes due, is recorded by an administrator, then confirmed by the traveler

## Slide 7

### Privacy is part of trust

- Marketplace cards show useful route and service details, not private documents or addresses
- Private storage and scoped access protect verification, proof, and payout assets
- Authorized review supports safety without public oversharing

## Slide 8

### China-first, globally responsible

- Built for routes originating from or connecting with China
- Members are warned to describe goods accurately and comply with customs, airline, carrier, and destination requirements
- BridgeX does not claim customs approval or legal clearance

## Slide 9

### A 5% service fee requires clear records

- The fee is disclosed inside the protected payment experience
- Future implementation should record gross amount, fee, net payout, status, and any reversal
- Clear transaction records reduce operational ambiguity

## Slide 10

### Built now. Strengthen before scale.

- Public marketplace, post flows, offers/interests, protected matches, live-style messaging, review, reports, verification, payments, and payout tracking
- Member, admin, and super-admin surfaces
- 55 automated checks covering key business rules
- Formal fee ledger and payment reconciliation
- Stronger data retention, access logging, and category risk controls
- Admin queue assignment, service-level monitoring, and mobile release gates

## Slide 11

### Build the trusted route, not just the marketplace

BridgeX is designed to make cross-border carrying more visible, accountable, and manageable.

Use verified members. Describe goods accurately. Follow every applicable law and carrier rule.
